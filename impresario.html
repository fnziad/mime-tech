Artist Biography

Master Shariar Shawon stands as a pioneering figure in contemporary Bangladeshi mime, bridging traditional performance arts with international corporeal theatre traditions. Trained in the lineage of Étienne Decroux and influenced by the indigenous performance cultures of Bengal, Shawon has developed a unique pedagogical approach that synthesizes Huda Yoga breathing techniques with corporeal mime methodology.

His international touring career has taken him to prestigious venues across South Korea, Morocco, India, and throughout Southeast Asia, where he has both performed and conducted master classes. In 2018, he founded the Shawon Mime Academy, dedicated to training the next generation of Bangladeshi mime artists using techniques documented in Dr. Fr. Tapon De Rozario's seminal work, "Echoes of Silence."

Shawon's artistic practice is deeply rooted in the theatre of resistance, using the body as a tool for social commentary and cultural preservation. His work during the July 2024 Uprising demonstrated the power of physical theatre as a form of peaceful protest, documenting key moments through the art of mime.
